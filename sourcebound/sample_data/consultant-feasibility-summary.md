# Feasibility study summary: Riverside Library

*Fictional sample document for the Sourcebound demo. Alderbrook is an invented town.*

Prepared by Harrow & Pike Building Consultants for the Alderbrook Office of Community Facilities.

## Cost estimate

We estimate the total renovation cost at $4.8 million, which includes a 12% contingency for unknown conditions in the older parts of the building. The estimate excludes furniture and new library stock.

## Programme

Construction will take 22 months to complete. The longer programme reflects phased work around the 1962 wing and lead times for the lift and heat pump equipment.

## Risks

Asbestos is likely present in ceiling tiles of the 1962 wing and must be removed by licensed contractors before other work starts. Other risks include roof structure decay and rising steel prices.

## Energy performance

Heat pumps, triple glazing and roof insulation should reduce energy use by 30 to 35 percent compared with the existing gas boilers.

## Recommendation

We recommend a phased approach so that the children's area can reopen first.
