Residents back library renovation, survey finds
Fictional sample document for the Sourcebound demo. Alderbrook is an invented town.

Alderbrook Gazette, local news.

A town survey of 1,240 respondents found that 68% of residents support renovating the Riverside Library. The council's briefing memo puts the price of the renovation at $4.2 million.

The library will be closed while work is under way. Library staff said a temporary branch would open at the Millbrook Community Centre, offering children's story time twice a week and free computer access.

Some residents raised concerns about parking near the temporary branch and about losing the library's quiet study space during exams.

The council is scheduled to vote on the renovation plan on 12 November.
