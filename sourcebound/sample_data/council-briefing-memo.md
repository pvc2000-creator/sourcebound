# Council briefing memo: Riverside Library renovation

*Fictional sample document for the Sourcebound demo. Alderbrook is an invented town.*

Prepared for the Alderbrook Town Council by the Office of Community Facilities.

## Summary

The Riverside Library renovation will cost $4.2 million and modernise the 1962 building for the next thirty years. Construction is expected to last 18 months, starting in March 2027 if the council approves the plan.

## Funding

Sixty percent of the renovation cost will come from a municipal bond. A state library grant will cover 25% of the cost, and the remaining 15% will come from private donations raised by the Friends of Riverside Library.

## Scope

The renovation replaces the roof, adds a step-free entrance and a lift, and expands the children's area. New heat pumps and insulation will reduce the library's energy use by 35% compared with the current gas boilers.

## Service during construction

The library will close to the public during construction. A temporary branch with a reduced collection will operate from the Millbrook Community Centre, and the home delivery service for housebound readers will continue.
